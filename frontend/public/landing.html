Observation Challenge

Examination halls generate more movement than an invigilator can continuously interpret.

Manual monitoring makes sustained observation difficult. Short glances, body turns, unusual hand movements and coordinated behaviour can be missed or noticed too late.

Simultaneous 360° temporal memory for 40+ candidates exceeds human observation bandwidth.

Micro-Glances & Transient Turning

A 1.5-second glance towards a neighbour's desk occurs between invigilator sweep cycles without persistent record.

Coordinated Spatial Passing

Synchronized motion anomalies and shared glance vectors across adjacent rows are difficult to verify manually.

Absence of Objective Proof

Subjective recollections cause inconclusive disputes without verifiable, timestamped anatomical tracking.
